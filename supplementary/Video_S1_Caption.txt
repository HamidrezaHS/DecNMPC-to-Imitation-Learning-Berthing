Supplementary Video S1. Numerical replay and method overview for decentralized imitation learning of tug-assisted tanker berthing. A short introductory panel shows the shared measured ship state, local independent neural specialists, environment-dependent routing, and local progress/approach supervision. Recorded five-vessel ship/tug trajectories and towline endpoints are replayed side by side for the reference Dec-NMPC and proposed decentralized IL in the two matched development cases with wind/current toward-directions 90°/90° and 270°/270°. Each panel uses the same physical coordinate frame. The time labels refer to simulated time, accelerated for viewing; smooth intermediate poses are linearly interpolated from logged numerical observations, not independently simulated. The video is not field footage or experimental validation. The figures show first accepted berthing states, not post-berthing station keeping.
